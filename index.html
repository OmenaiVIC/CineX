The Opportunity

Africa’s $5B+ Creative Economy, Untapped

Nollywood alone produces 2,500+ films per year, yet fewer than 5% of productions access formal financing.

Film, music, gaming, and immersive media are invisible to the full spectrum of capital providers:

LPs VCs Venture Studios Fund Managers Angel Investors Family Offices
— all lacking verifiable production data and trusted disbursement rails.

The Problem

  • No verifiable milestone tracking — capital gets burned without proof of work
  • Escrow is opaque; idle funds earn zero yield
  • Creative IP has no standardized onboarding for investors
  • Web2 crowdfunding takes 8–12% + leaves no asset trail

The CineX Solution

  • Verified intake via Stacks-based identity & portfolio proofs
  • Milestone-gated payouts — capital released on verified delivery
  • Yield-bearing escrow — idle capital earns yield via Stacking pools
  • 5% flat escrow fee vs. 8–12% incumbent — transparent & auditable
How It Works

Three Layers of Trust

1

Verified Intake

  • Creatives onboard via Stacks identity proofs
  • Portfolio verification through on‑chain credentials
  • Social‑graph endorsements from verified peers
  • No anonymous listings — every creator is authenticated
2

Milestone Gating

  • Capital released in tranches against pre‑agreed deliverables
  • Milestones: script delivery, principal photography, post‑production
  • On‑chain verification of milestone completion
  • Transparent, auditable disbursement trail
3

Productive Escrow

  • Idle escrow capital earns 4–7% estimated APY
  • Yield via Bitcoin‑secured DeFi: Stacks stacking & sBTC lending
  • 70% of earned yield passes to creators & capital providers
  • CineX takes 30% performance fee (standard DeFi model)
  • Yield‑share model protects against market volatility
